# HotM 2026.9.5

HotM 2026.9.5 adds producer-owned realtime contract verification. All 48 events
are checked through EventSource, fetch-SSE, canonical WebSocket envelopes and
the existing anonymous-local legacy WebSocket payload path. The fixtures come
from Fortemi's Rust producer at the existing pinned live authority revision;
they are not inferred from event names or generated solely from schemas.

CI verifies the producer receipt, manifest, individual fixture bytes, corpus
digest and schema/envelope revisions. Its transport receipt records all 48
events on each path. Existing negative mutations and unknown-event tests remain
in place, including preservation of raw unknown payloads and rejection of
malformed known events. See [the integration ADR](../../.aiwg/architecture/adr/ADR-010-fortemi-v2026-07-api-coverage.md)
for the evidence boundaries.

This is a verification release, not a changed realtime wire protocol. Hosted
admission remains Fortemi Auth `v2026.9.0`, claim contract `1.1.0`, profile
`rust-node-jwt-v1`; the packaged desktop sidecar and live authority pins are
unchanged. Mocked transport tests do not establish live hosted authorization,
Windows qualification or suite-wide portability. Knowledge Shard claims remain
profile-specific and the suite audit remains `NO-GO`.
